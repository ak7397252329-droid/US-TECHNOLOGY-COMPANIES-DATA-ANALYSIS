👥 HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview
A complete HR Analytics solution built with Power BI to analyze workforce demographics, attrition, retention, and organizational trends. This dashboard helps HR leaders make data-driven decisions to build a better workplace.

This project was executed as part of my MBA specialization in HR & Business Analytics at Bangalore University.

🎯 Business Problem
Organizations often struggle with:
- High attrition in specific departments
- Lack of visibility into workforce diversity & tenure
- No clear view of long-term employee growth

This dashboard answers: *Who is leaving? Where? Why? And what can we do?*

📊 Dataset
- *Total Records:* 17.8K Employees
- *Fields:* Employee ID, Gender, Age, Race, Department, Location (Headquarter/Remote), Employment Status, Termination Reason, Hire Date
- *Time Span:* 2000 - 2020

🔧 Tools & Tech Stack
Tool | Purpose
**Power BI** | Dashboard & Visualization
**Power Query** | Data Cleaning & Transformation
**DAX** | KPI Measures (Total Employees, Avg Tenure, Attrition Rate)
**Excel** | Data Pre-processing
📈 Dashboard Highlights

*1. Executive Summary*
- Total Employees: 17,803
- Male: 9,339 | Female: 8,460 | Non-Conforming: 4
- Average Length of Employment: 8.44 Years

*2. Attrition Analysis*
- Highest Termination Rate: *Auditing (19%)*
- Followed by Legal & Training Departments
- Key Insight: Need for targeted retention programs in these units

*3. Demographics & Diversity*
- Age Distribution: 62% workforce in 25-54 age group
- Location: 75% Headquarter, 25% Remote
- Race & Gender Diversity visualized for DEI initiatives

*4. Trend Analysis*
- Change in Employee Numbers (2000-2020)
- Steady growth after 2005, stable retention post 2015

💡 Key Insights & Recommendations
✅ Auditing dept needs immediate attention - high churn
✅ Average tenure of 8.44 years indicates good employee loyalty
✅ Gender ratio is well-balanced, promote more inclusive hiring
✅ Focus on remote workforce engagement strategies
